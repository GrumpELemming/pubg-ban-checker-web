Privacy Policy

Discord sign-in is optional. You can continue using a browser-saved Watchlist without an account. If you sign in, we use the minimum Discord profile information needed to identify your account and sync your Watchlist across devices.

Effective and last updated: 22 August 2026

1. Who we are

PUBG Ban Checker is an independently operated PUBG utility. For privacy questions, account-data requests, or deletion requests, email thegrumpylemming@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers pubgbanchecker.com, including the optional Discord sign-in and cloud Watchlist sync. Discord separately controls the information in your Discord account and processes it under Discord's Privacy Policy.

3. Information we process

Discord account information

We request only Discord's identify OAuth scope. Discord returns basic profile information, but we retain only your:

  • Discord user ID;
  • Discord username; and
  • Discord display name.

We do not request or store your Discord email address, password, guild or server memberships, friends, messages, linked accounts, or payment information. Discord may include an avatar reference in its basic profile response, but PUBG Ban Checker does not store or use it.

Synced Watchlist information

When you enable account sync, we store Watchlist information associated with your Discord user ID, including:

  • platform and PUBG player names or account IDs;
  • clan, ban-status, last-check, and name-history information;
  • notes you choose to add; and
  • record identifiers, revision numbers, and creation or update timestamps used for syncing.

Do not place sensitive personal information in Watchlist notes.

Authentication and security information

We create an opaque session token and an anti-forgery token. Only cryptographic hashes of these tokens are stored in the database, along with session creation, last-use, and expiry times. Discord's temporary OAuth access token is used once to retrieve your basic profile and is not saved.

Browser storage and service logs

Guest Watchlists, signed-in cache copies, sync state, theme, language, and selected platform can be stored locally in your browser. Our hosting and security providers may also process routine technical information such as IP address, request time, requested path, browser user-agent, and security headers for delivery, rate limiting, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.

4. Why we use this information

We use the information described above only to:

  • sign you in and show which Discord account is connected;
  • save, merge, protect, and synchronise your Watchlist across devices;
  • check the public PUBG player and ban information you request;
  • maintain session security, prevent abuse, and resolve sync conflicts; and
  • diagnose errors and keep the service reliable.

We do not sell your information, use Discord information for advertising, profile your relationships, or send marketing messages to your Discord account.

Where UK data-protection law applies, account and Watchlist processing is necessary to provide the optional service you request. Limited security and operational processing is based on our legitimate interest in protecting and maintaining the service. We process information when legally required to do so.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use only the following account cookies. They are strictly necessary when you request Discord sign-in or a signed-in session; they are not advertising or analytics cookies.

Cookie Purpose Lifetime
pbc_oauth_state Prevents forged Discord login callbacks. 10 minutes
pbc_oauth_next Returns you safely to the Watchlist after sign-in. 10 minutes
pbc_session Maintains your signed-in session using an opaque token. Up to 30 days
pbc_csrf Protects Watchlist changes and sign-out requests. Up to 30 days

Account cookies are marked Secure and HttpOnly. Browser local storage remains on that device until you clear the site's storage or remove the entries. Disabling storage may prevent guest Watchlists, preferences, or offline cache features from working.

6. Service providers and sharing

We disclose information only where needed to operate the service:

  • Discord provides OAuth sign-in and your basic profile response.
  • Cloudflare proxies and protects API traffic.
  • Render hosts the application backend.
  • Neon hosts the PostgreSQL database used for accounts, sessions, and synced Watchlists.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the static website files.
  • PUBG's public API receives the platform and PUBG player identifiers required for checks. We do not send your Discord identity to the PUBG API.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights. We do not sell or rent personal information.

7. International processing

The website uses global providers, and the account backend and database are hosted in Oregon, United States. Information may therefore be processed outside your country. Where UK data-protection law applies, international processing must be covered by an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as provider contractual protections. Contact us if you would like more information.

8. How long we keep information

  • Discord OAuth access tokens are discarded immediately after the login profile request.
  • OAuth state cookies expire after 10 minutes.
  • Sessions stop being accepted after no more than 30 days; expired session records are removed during database cleanup.
  • Discord identity fields and synced Watchlists are kept while the sync account remains in use, until deletion is requested, or until the service is discontinued.
  • Operational logs and backups are retained for limited periods determined by security needs and provider configuration.
  • Browser-saved data remains until you clear it on that device.

9. Your choices and deletion requests

Discord sign-in is optional. Signing out ends the current server session but does not delete the synced Watchlist or account record. You can also remove the application through Discord's authorised-app settings; sign out of PUBG Ban Checker as well to end the current website session.

To request a copy, correction, or deletion of your Discord-linked account and synced Watchlists, email thegrumpylemming@gmail.com with the subject “PUBG Ban Checker data request”. We may ask you to verify control of the relevant Discord account. Never send your Discord password, OAuth code, session cookie, or database credentials.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal data. In the UK, you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

10. Security

We minimise the Discord information retained, use HTTPS, protect account cookies from JavaScript access, hash session and anti-forgery tokens in the database, restrict write requests, and do not retain Discord access tokens. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please report suspected problems to the contact address above.

11. Age limits

Do not use Discord sign-in if you are below Discord's minimum permitted age in your country. We do not knowingly use Discord API data belonging to children below the legally permitted age. Contact us if you believe such information has been submitted.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, providers, or legal requirements change. The date at the top will show the latest revision. Material changes affecting Discord-linked information will be made clearly available from the Watchlist sign-in area.

This notice is intended to explain the service's actual data handling in clear language. It is not a substitute for legal advice about obligations that may apply to the operator in a particular jurisdiction.
