About PUBG Ban Checker

PUBG Ban Checker lets you check the ban status of PUBG accounts on Steam, Xbox, PlayStation and Kakao using a player name or account ID. You can add accounts to a watchlist and re-check them one at a time, with optional Discord sign-in to save your watchlist across devices. Ban results reflect the information returned by PUBG at the time of each check.
